How to play

    
    
      
  • Move: WASD / arrow keys, or tap/click the ground.
    • 
      
  • E — Gather / Deposit: pick berries 🫐, chop wood 🪵, fish 🐟 at the shore, deposit at the Granary 🏚️, or beat back a wildfire.
    • 
      
  • Q — Take food from the Granary. Agents who see you will remember. Bruin will remember next match too.
    • 
      
  • F — Eat from your basket. G — Gift food to the nearest player: gifts build trust.
    • 
      
  • T — Talk (or 🎙️ speak): “who is the traitor?”, “do you trust me?”, “help me”, “call a council”, “I'm hungry”.
    • 
      
  • The traitor is one of the animals (random each match). It steals when nobody is looking and may start a wildfire. Marigold the owl calls a Council when trust collapses — everyone votes to exile.
    • 
      
  • Win: the Granary hits its food & wood goals by sunset. Lose: the traitor fills its hidden stash, or the village starves.
    • 
      
  • The ledger shows every agent decision with its utility scores. The traitor's secret decisions are sealed with a SHA-256 commitment and unsealed at the end — proof the AI never cheated.
